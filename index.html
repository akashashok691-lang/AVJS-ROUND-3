About the timetable data

Attendance uses the 10 sections embedded in the Round 1 source. The room locator uses 13 schedule records embedded in the Round 2 source. The contest text says 10 sections, while the room source contains 13 and mixes 2024–25 first-year data with 2026–27 schedules. The supplied sources do not identify one authoritative version. Room capacities and amenities are estimates; no room is reserved by this app. Tailwind CSS and Chart.js are optional CDN dependencies; calculations and room search continue without them, and the chart panel reports when Chart.js is unavailable.
