Timetable and dependency notes

Attendance uses 10 sections in the Round 1 source. Room lookup uses 13 schedule records in the Round 2 source; the brief says 10, and the room source mixes first-year 2024–25 with 2026–27 schedules. The sources do not establish the authoritative version. Room capacities and amenities are estimates; unmapped venues and unsupplied bookings are not represented. Tailwind CSS and Chart.js load from CDNs; no backend or AI API is used. Calculations and room matching run locally.
